Core Objectives & Features

1. Digital Wardrobe: Users upload photos to digitize & organize their closet.
2. Smart Suggestions: Recommends outfits based on weather, temperature, and occasions.
3. AI Style Generator: Natural language styling matching user moods & vibes.
4. Mix and Match: Visual combinations of existing clothes with new items from online partner stores.
5. Shopping Links & Partnerships: Monetization via affiliate partnerships with top fashion retailers.
